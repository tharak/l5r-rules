The rules snapshot was imported from [Last Haiku](https://lasthaiku.wikidot.com/), which states a [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/) license. Imported content retains that license. Legend of the Five Rings belongs to its respective owners. This document is excluded from deployment.

Structured weapon and armor statistics and skill mastery descriptions reuse the resource catalogs in the user's [LegendOfTheFiveRings core library](https://github.com/tharak/LegendOfTheFiveRings/tree/main/Sources/LegendOfTheFiveRings/Resources). Ability, ancestor, and supplementary school descriptions come from the source pages below. Rules calculations were checked against the fourth-edition printed core rulebook; the source PDF is excluded from deployment.

The seven Great Clan mons in `public/assets/clans/official/` (Crab, Crane, Dragon, Lion, Phoenix, Scorpion, Unicorn) are official artwork from the clan pages of [legendofthefiverings.com](https://www.legendofthefiverings.com/lore/clans/the-crane-clan/) (© Legend of the Five Rings, published by Fantasy Flight Games / Asmodee; all rights reserved). They are used for non-commercial fan purposes, resized to 192px from the site's `05a_Clan_Icon` images on `cdn.svc.asmodee.net`, and are not covered by the CC BY-SA license above.

The Mantis, Spider, and Imperial Family mons in `public/assets/clans/wiki/` come from [l5r.wiki](https://l5r.wiki/clans) (`/mons/mon_mantis.gif`, `/mons/mon_spider.gif`, `/mons/mon_w_imperial_family.png`), whose content is licensed [CC BY-SA](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). They were recoloured (Mantis green, Spider black, Imperial gold), trimmed, and resized to 192px; the modified files are shared under the same license.

The Badger, Bat, Boar, Dragonfly, Falcon, Fox, Hare, Monkey, Ox, Raven, Snake, Sparrow, Tiger, and Tortoise mons, plus the Nezumi mon and an alternative Mantis mon (`nezumi.webp`, `mantis.webp`), in `public/assets/clans/` were cut from ["The Clans of Rokugan"](https://www.deviantart.com/shin-ian/art/The-Clans-of-Rokugan-721225095) by Shin-Ian (© 2017 Shin-Ian), a black-and-white mandala of clan mons. Each mon was masked from the mandala, made transparent, inked dark brown, and resized to 192px. The artwork's mons are unlabelled; the Badger, Hare, Sparrow, and Tiger identifications are best guesses.

When several sources have a mon for the same clan, the app uses them in this order: the official site, l5r.wiki, the mandala, then l5r.fandom.com (`MON_PRIORITY` in `character-catalog.js`).
The five-rings mark `public/assets/l5r-mark.webp` (site logo, favicon, and the mon for clans no source has one for) is the official Legend of the Five Rings favicon from [legendofthefiverings.com](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-l5r/uploads/image-converter/2025/05/L5R-Favicon_05-16-25.webp), resized to 256px.
The map of Rokugan behind the campaign pages (`public/assets/rokugan-map.webp`) is the official map background from [legendofthefiverings.com](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-l5r/uploads/image-converter/2023/06/03_Map_Background-Rokugan-1900x2755.webp), resized to 1600px wide.
The Great Clan cover art in `public/assets/clans/covers/` comes from the clan pages of [legendofthefiverings.com](https://www.legendofthefiverings.com/lore/clans/the-crane-clan/): the wide `01_Clan_Cover` images (resized from 1900px to 1200px wide) and the tall `UI_L5R_Clan_Cover_<Clan>_Mobile` images (780×2000, unchanged), saved as `<clan>.webp` and `<clan>-tall.webp`.
The Great Clan family art in `public/assets/clans/families/` comes from the clan pages of legendofthefiverings.com (`04<letter>_Clan_Families-<n>-900x900.webp`), resized to 480px. The site does not label them; the family each one represents is matched by its content (`FAMILY_ART` in `character-catalog.js`).
The Great Clan banners in `public/assets/clans/banners/` (`01_Clans_Page_<Clan>-1.webp`, resized from 1000×2000 to 500×1000) and the clans overview cover `public/assets/clans/covers/clans.webp` / `clans-tall.webp` (`00_Clans_Cover-1-1900x1781.webp` resized to 1200px, `UI_L5R_Lore_Cover_Clans-1.webp` unchanged) also come from legendofthefiverings.com.
The clan illustrations in `public/assets/clans/illustrations/` (`03<letter>_Clan_Squared_Illustrations-<n>-1200x1200.webp`, resized to 600px) and the Shadowlands and Realm of Rokugan images in `public/assets/lore/` (mon, covers, maps, illustrations, wiki thumbnails, and the landscape background, resized) also come from legendofthefiverings.com. The Shadowlands/Realm grouping of the illustrations is based on their content.
